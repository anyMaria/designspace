Hover a photo → name below, thought bubble at the top-right

The name appears under the item after a short hover. The bubble sits just outside the top-right corner (three dots = it already has a description). Click it and it opens into a panel as tall as the photo (min 320 px wide), with the long description and a summary of the details.

Connection lines stop at the edges, never cross the pictures

Each line is cut where it meets the edge of each picture (on the side facing the other item), with a 6 px gap, so nothing is drawn across the pictures and lines fan out naturally. Parallel lines (one per shared criterion) stay side by side.

Minimap with connections → double-click for the Overview

The minimap shows items as tiny coloured shapes and draws My connections plus the lines of whatever you hover or select. Double-click it (or press O) to open the Overview: the whole space at a glance, every item as a tiny thumbnail, every connection drawn (shared values meet at labelled stars, like tags in Obsidian).
